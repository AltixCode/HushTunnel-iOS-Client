## Interpretation and caveat

Verisign is the authoritative registry for `.com`. An HTTP 404 means no registration object exists at lookup time, but it does not guarantee that a registrar will sell the name: a name can be reserved, restricted, premium-priced, or registered moments later. Availability also does not establish trademark rights. Confirm the final choice at a registrar and perform jurisdiction-appropriate trademark clearance before launch.
