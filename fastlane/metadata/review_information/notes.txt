Thank you for your review. We have addressed all previous feedback, added native in-app subscriptions, and implemented full VPN compliance:

1. CORE FUNCTIONALITY & NETWORK EXTENSION:
HushTunnel is a dedicated VPN application whose core and primary functionality is providing a secure device-level VPN tunnel. It utilizes Apple's NetworkExtension framework (NEVPNManager, NETunnelProviderManager, and NEPacketTunnelProvider).

2. PROMINENT VPN DISCLOSURE (Guideline 5.4):
Before any VPN tunnel is established and before any purchase controls are shown, the app displays a prominent, separate VPN disclosure screen. It clearly explains:
- Encrypted device-to-endpoint tunnel boundary.
- Transient processing of network packets without persistent storage.
- Strict zero-log policy: we do NOT log, monitor, or retain browsing history, DNS queries, traffic destinations, or payload content.
- VPN traffic is NEVER sold, used for ads, or disclosed to third parties.
- The user must explicitly tap "Agree and Continue" to proceed. Tapping "Not Now" starts no tunnel and returns the user to the sign-in screen.

3. IN-APP PURCHASES & SUBSCRIPTIONS (Guideline 3.1.1 & 3.1.2):
All plans and services are now available as native auto-renewable Apple In-App Purchases (processed via RevenueCat). Subscriptions can be browsed and purchased natively within the app.
- Available subscriptions: HushTunnel 1 Month, HushTunnel 3 Months, and HushTunnel 12 Months.
- "Restore Purchases" is prominently accessible in Account Settings.
- "Manage App Store Subscription" links directly to the user's Apple ID subscription management page.
- "Delete Account Permanently" is fully supported in Account Settings.
