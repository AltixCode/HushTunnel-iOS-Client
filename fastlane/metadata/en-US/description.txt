HushTunnel is a privacy-first, secure device-level VPN and edge network tunnel. VPN connectivity is HushTunnel's core and primary functionality, utilizing Apple's NetworkExtension framework to deliver end-to-end encrypted transit from your iOS device to global high-performance edge nodes.

Whether browsing on public Wi-Fi or cellular networks, HushTunnel protects your personal data and network traffic from interception, throttling, and surveillance.

KEY FEATURES:
• Secure Device-Level VPN: Encrypted tunnel routes your device traffic safely through dedicated multi-node edge infrastructure.
• Strict Zero-Log Privacy: We do not log, track, or monitor browsing history, DNS queries, traffic destinations, or packet payloads.
• Intelligent Routing: Fast, low-latency connections across global edge locations.
• Prominent Disclosure: Transparent in-app privacy and data processing disclosure shown before any connection is established.
• Native Account Controls: In-app subscription management, purchase restoration, and permanent account deletion.
