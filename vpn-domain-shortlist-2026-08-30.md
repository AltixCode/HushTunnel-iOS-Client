## Why only 103 names have definitive results

The token is attached to a new free account. WhoisJSON returned this exact explanation when the cap was reached: “Your new free account is limited to 100 requests during the first 48 hours. The full monthly quota unlocks after that.” The result count is 103 because some responses appear not to have been charged (for example, cached lookups); the API's own `Remaining-Requests` header reached zero.

The service also limits the free tier to 20 requests per minute. Temporary 429 responses were retried only after pacing the accepted requests below that limit. The full 1,000-candidate list remains in the CSV, with unchecked rows explicitly labeled `unchecked-account-cap`.

## Naming recommendation

**BlinkVeil** is the strongest consumer VPN brand here. It is easy to say and type, feels modern, and compresses the two core promises into one image: fast (`Blink`) and private (`Veil`). **BlinkCloak** is the strongest alternative if you want a more overt security feel. **ArcCloak** is the compact, technical option.

## Important caveat

Domain availability is time-sensitive and does not establish trademark rights. Register the preferred domain promptly, then run a proper trademark search in the countries where the VPN will be sold before investing in identity, apps, or advertising.
